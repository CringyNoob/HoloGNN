# Methods

## Architecture and Experimental Setup
The Holo-GNN architecture functions as a structure-centric sequence encoder. In this implementation, the base embeddings are produced by a pre-trained ESM-2 language model (`facebook/esm2_t6_8M_UR50D`). This approach tokenizes sequence variants up to a maximum length of 100 tokens, isolating localized conformational signatures and amino-acid level interactions. Output embeddings originating from the ESM-2 backbone are feature-pooled via averaging across the sequence dimension, resulting in a condensed 320-dimensional feature vector. These representations are subsequently passed into task-specific predictive heads.

## Downstream Task 1: Protein Stability Prediction
To assess foundational applicability, Holo-GNN was applied to regression-based protein stability prediction. The stability predictive head maps averaged sequence representations to continuous experimental stability values. Model performance was assessed by measuring the Pearson correlation coefficient ($R$) between the network's predictive stability outputs and normalized experimental ground truth stabilities.

## Downstream Task 2: Structural Disease Classification
A distinct predictive module, evaluating molecular pathogenicity, was configured to parse structurally disruptive mutations. We assessed structural disease diagnostic competency within a binary classification framework. A mock structural mutation test dataset ($N=500$) was systematically generated by injecting random single-point mutations into a base 74-residue protein sequence. High-propensity, main-chain bending restrictions mimicking deleterious mutations, specifically Proline and Glycine substitutions, were explicitly parameterized as pathogenic alterations (label 1.0). Conversely, structurally agnostic, isosteric, and aliphatic substitutions (Alanine, Valine, Isoleucine, Leucine) served as benign (label 0.0) substitutions. 

The proteomics classification head—a single linear feedforward layer—ingested the pooled sequence embeddings, outputting preliminary logits. These outputs were translated to pseudo-probabilities via a sigmoid activation function. For hard categorical delineation, a binary decision boundary of $p > 0.5$ divided the pathogenic variants from the benign subset. Standard statistical classification metrics including diagnostic accuracy, a confusion matrix, and Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC-AUC) curves were utilized for validation against this independently generated test cohort.

# Results

## Overview of Model Efficacy
Evaluation across downstream tasks suggests that Holo-GNN effectively internalizes critical topological paradigms strictly from its modified structural-sequence formulation. It demonstrates a resilient discriminatory profile for delineating distinct pathogenic structural classes, accompanying its generalized monotonic tracking capability in modeling stability values.

## Stability Validation
Figure 1 documents the model's regression inferences charted alongside ground-truth experimental stability values. The predictive algorithm demonstrated a moderate, dependable monotonic association with stability outcomes, acquiring a Pearson Correlation $R = 0.4071$. Though Holo-GNN reliably captures broad directional tendencies matching the idealized theoretical trace ($y=x$) throughout the core functional envelope, the scatterplot distribution reveals minor heteroscedasticity and a systematic bias toward conservative underprediction at extreme positive stability thresholds ($> 5.0$). 

## Diagnostic Competency in Predicting Pathogenic Function
Figure 2 outlines the uncalibrated classification performance mapped across a formal confusion statistical matrix for $N=500$ distinct mutations. The algorithm demonstrated an impressively stringent specificity index toward non-disruptive biological modifications. Out of 267 genuinely benign mutations instantiated in the dataset, 254 observations were accurately processed as expected True Negatives, netting a remarkably high testing Specificity of $95.1\%$. Correspondingly, amongst the 233 expected deleterious constraints, 90 true pathogenic substitutions were distinctly isolated, albeit at the cost of 143 False Negatives. In its default parameterization, the model is noticeably risk-averse—meaning it suppresses false positive alarms largely at the detriment of sensitivity—culminating in an aggregated diagnostic accuracy of $68.8\%$.

Despite the stringent discrete categorization outcomes, the underlying gradient of probabilistic discrimination produced by the model indicates substantial learning capability. The Receiver Operating Characteristic evaluation (Figure 3) highlights this robust discrimination potential decoupled from threshold mechanics, accumulating a substantial Area Under the Curve (AUC) of $0.84$. A rapid initial steepness plotted along the True Positive vertical axis demonstrates that the encoded representations maintain distinct, linearly separable manifolds isolating aggressive helix-breaking constraints from softer isosteric alterations, establishing that alternative optimal thresholds could safely push operational sensitivities significantly higher while retaining practical false alarm margins.
